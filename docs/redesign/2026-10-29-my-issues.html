My issues — three directions

All three keep what was decided: a page of its own, grouped by status, priority as a glyph instead of a word, and details on click in an inspector on the right — the same pattern as To-Do. What changes is what a row is for.

Shared rules: the status chip shows only when it says something the group doesn't (Escalated, Feedback, In Fixing, Backlog — not "In Progress" under In progress). One action per issue, in the inspector, instead of a button on every row. Session dots use the session status colors because they are session status. Data below is made up.

A · Quiet list

Closest to today, with the noise removed: one line, nothing on the right but the date. A dot before the title says a session exists (and its state); no dot, no session. Enter or double-click starts or opens it.
